## I-01 Locked Direction

### Preserve
- kobold pose
- spear
- corridor perspective
- tripwire
- pit trap
- wall torch
- overall scene concept

### Change
- reduce detail density roughly 30–40%
- remove most hatching
- reduce tiny scale texture
- reduce unnecessary stone cracks
- create larger clean white coloring areas
- lighter interior lines

### Avoid
- cute reinterpretation
- stick-figure simplification
- losing monster identity
- removing the trap
- adding gray shading
